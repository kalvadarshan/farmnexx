How it works

From the village mandi to your apartment in four steps

1

🏘️ Village Auction

An auction is held in each village on a fixed day and date. Small and large farmers sell to us.

2

🚚 Bulk Transport

Many small loads go in one truck, so transport costs less.

3

❄️ Cold Storage

Produce is stored cold in the city so it stays fresh and lasts longer.

4

📦 One-Day Delivery

We reach each apartment daily at a fixed time and serve many families at once.
